FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services and processes.

How can I book a test? ▾
Fill in the appointment form, WhatsApp us at 7082934628, or visit our lab at Bitna Road, Near Raunak Hotel, Pinjore. Contact us to confirm availability and opening hours.
Do you provide home sample collection? ▾
Home sample collection is available. Contact the lab to confirm service in your area, collection times, and any applicable charges.
When will I receive my reports? ▾
Report availability depends on the test. Contact the lab for the expected turnaround time and to request your reports on WhatsApp.
Are reports verified by doctors? ▾
Yes, all reports at Preet Diagnostic Lab are reviewed and verified by qualified pathologists before being released to patients. This ensures the highest accuracy and reliability of your results.
Which payment methods are accepted? ▾
We accept Cash, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking, and all major Debit/Credit Cards. For home collection, payment can be made to our staff at the time of sample collection or online in advance.
Do I need to fast before a blood test? ▾
Preparation depends on the test. Please contact the lab for fasting and preparation instructions when booking your appointment.
